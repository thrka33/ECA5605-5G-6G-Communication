# NB-IoT and LTE-M Performance Analysis Using MATLAB

## Overview

This project demonstrates the performance analysis of **NB-IoT (Narrowband Internet of Things)** and **LTE-M (Long Term Evolution for Machines)** using **MATLAB**. The simulation compares the performance of both IoT communication technologies in terms of **Throughput**, **Latency**, and **Packet Delivery Ratio (PDR)** under different **Signal-to-Noise Ratio (SNR)** values. The generated graphs provide a clear comparison of their communication performance.

---

## Objectives

1. **To analyze the throughput performance** of NB-IoT and LTE-M under different SNR values.

2. **To compare the latency** of NB-IoT and LTE-M communication systems.

3. **To evaluate the Packet Delivery Ratio (PDR)** of NB-IoT and LTE-M for different channel conditions.

## Result

The MATLAB simulation successfully compares the performance of NB-IoT and LTE-M under varying SNR conditions. The results show that **LTE-M provides significantly higher throughput and lower latency**, making it suitable for applications requiring faster communication. **NB-IoT offers reliable packet delivery with lower bandwidth requirements**, making it ideal for low-power, wide-area IoT applications. Both technologies demonstrate improved Packet Delivery Ratio as SNR increases.
